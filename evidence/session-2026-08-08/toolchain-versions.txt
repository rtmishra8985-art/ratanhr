CONCLUSION: the blocker is not merely "wrong SDK patch version" — this sandbox's network
egress allowlist (see filesystem/network configuration) does not include api.nuget.org,
dot.net, builds.dotnet.microsoft.com, registry-1.docker.io, docker.io, mcr.microsoft.com, or
any MySQL/Playwright-browser download host. archive.ubuntu.com and security.ubuntu.com are
reachable (that is how dotnet-sdk-8.0 8.0.129 itself was installed), as is registry.npmjs.org.
Even with a fully installed local SDK, `dotnet restore` cannot reach NuGet, so `dotnet build`,
`dotnet test`, and `dotnet ef` cannot proceed regardless of which SDK patch is present.
