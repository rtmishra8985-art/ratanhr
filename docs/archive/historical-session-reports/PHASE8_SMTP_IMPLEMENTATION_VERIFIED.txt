Retry Logic             Automatic       ✅          Failed email retry
Dead Letter Queue       Hangfire        ✅          Failed job tracking

FLOW:
1. EmailService.SendAsync() called
   ↓
2. Message queued to Redis
   ↓
3. EmailQueueWorker picks up job
   ↓
4. Attempts SMTP delivery
   ↓
5. Success → Job complete
   Failure → Auto-retry (configured intervals)
